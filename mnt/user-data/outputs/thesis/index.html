E-Commerce Purchase Intention — SEM Study

SPSS · SPSS AMOS · R n = 384 participants EFA → CFA → SEM pipeline ESPE · Ecuador · 2021

Undergraduate thesis investigating factors influencing e-commerce purchase intention during COVID-19 in Quito, Ecuador. Applied a full EFA → CFA → SEM pipeline across 5 latent constructs. Final model: CFI 0.979, RMSEA 0.046 — exceeding all goodness-of-fit thresholds. Social Influence and Facilitating Conditions significantly predict Habit; Performance Expectancy does not (p = .333).
