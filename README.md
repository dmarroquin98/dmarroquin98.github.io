## 📌 Project Overview

End-to-end planning and financial feasibility analysis for a large-scale **industrial real estate development** in Ecuador, comprising warehouses (*bodegas*), office-warehouses (*ofibodegas*), commercial areas, trailer parking, and a wastewater treatment plant — totalling **165,757 m²** of land.

This project was developed from initial concept through financial close, covering scope definition, architectural planning, financial modeling, break-even analysis, and investor cash flow evaluation.

## 🗂️ Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)

The project was structured into 5 major phases:

1. **Planning & Design** — Feasibility study, ROI analysis, environmental impact, architectural & structural design, permitting, scheduling and budget allocation
2. **Site Preparation** — Land clearing, grading, access roads, fencing, excavation and drainage
3. **Construction** — Foundation, warehouse and office-warehouse structures, commercial areas, trailer infrastructure, wastewater plant, MEP systems (electrical, plumbing, HVAC), final finishes
4. **Quality & Compliance** — Structural testing, regulatory compliance, preliminary handover
5. **Project Closeout** — Final site preparation, client delivery, administrative and financial close

---

## 📐 Pre-Sale Financing Strategy

The project was designed around an **off-plan sales model** (*venta en plano*), a common structured financing approach in Latin American real estate development:

- **30–50%** of capital funded through pre-sales (buyer deposits during construction)
- **50–70%** financed through bank credit or private investors
- Pre-sales serve as proof-of-demand, reducing lender risk and enabling project kick-off before full construction funding is secured

This structure mirrors approaches used in **Canadian real estate development** (pre-construction condos, strata developments), making the methodology directly transferable.
